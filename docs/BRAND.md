# infine. brand system

Built from the drop 01 campaign posters (`assets/brand/`).

## Who it's for

Gen Z buying their first real diamond, and then their next one. Fine jewellery without the "save it for a special occasion" energy.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ink | `#111111` | ground, text on colour blocks, the poster button |
| lime | `#D7FF3A` | the loudest block, handles, countdown digits, primary buy button on ink |
| pink | `#FD4FA6` | second line of headlines, "x left" stickers, pay button, the dot in `infine.` |
| lilac | `#CBB5FE` | soft block: first diamond, the vault, upcoming drop stickers |
| ice | `#A9D8FF` | the mascot's body, one stone on the orbit |
| white | `#FFFFFF` | text on ink, after dark block |

Sections are full-bleed colour blocks stacked like story posters. Never put lime text on lilac or pink on lime.

## Type

- **Display**: Montserrat Alternates 800, always lowercase, tight tracking (-0.045em), line height 0.86. The single-storey alternates are the brand's voice.
- **Body/UI**: Montserrat 500-700.
- **Mono**: Space Mono for prices, small talk and meta ("ear party / starts at / AED 1,450").
- **Pixel**: Silkscreen for countdowns and "main character energy".

## Graphics

- `infine.` wordmark with a pink full stop.
- `@infine` handle in lime opens most blocks.
- Pixel diamond (white facets, pink centre line, ink outline).
- Smiley diamond mascot (ice blue, blush cheeks). Use for empty states and confirmations.
- Dotted orbit with stones riding it (white, ice, pink). Use behind ear party and the hero.
- Tennis lines rendered as product art when there's no photo.

## Voice

Lowercase, short, a bit cheeky, never mean. Say what it is, then make it fun.

- stack it. mix it. make it yours.
- your first diamond. not your mom's.
- main character energy.
- gone is gone.

Avoid: "luxury", "exquisite", "timeless", fake urgency, fake discounts. Real scarcity only (edition sizes are real numbers).

## Drops

Numbered editions, early access for the list, public drop at 8pm Dubai, no restocks. Countdown and stock meter are always visible while a drop is live.
